A SMALL, OPEN BEGINNING

How Sprout works

Sprout is a no-key learning project. It searches Wikipedia when you ask a question, then builds a short answer from the pages it finds. Your saved notes can add personal context.

01Ask a questionSprout searches Wikipedia’s open encyclopedia.
02See where it came fromAnswers include links to the pages used.
03Teach it your contextYour notes are stored on this device only.
A note on “learning”
Sprout doesn’t train a large language model. Training something comparable to ChatGPT takes enormous datasets and computing. This app is a practical first step: open-source retrieval, simple answer composition, and memory you control.
